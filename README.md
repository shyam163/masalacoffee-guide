## 1. What you can change, and where

The admin panel lives at **https://masalacoffee.in/cms/admin/**.

The list down the left is the **sidebar**. It is grouped to match the website's
own menu, so the name you see on the website is the name you look for here.

![The sidebar, grouped the same way as the website menu](img/02-dashboard-sidebar.png)

*The sidebar. Click a group (Tour, News, Band…) to open it.*

| Website page | Sidebar entry |
| --- | --- |
| Tour dates | **Tour → Tour dates** |
| Tour names (e.g. "Kerala Tour 2026") | **Tour → Tour names** |
| News, Press, Features, fan stories | **News → News & stories** |
| Band members | **Band → Band members** |
| Releases, Songs & lyrics | **Music → Releases**, **Music → Songs & lyrics** |
| The music player in the corner of the site | **Music → Tracks (audio for the site player)** |
| Videos | **Media → Video collections**, **Media → Videos** |
| Photos | **Media → Photo albums**, **Media → Photos** |
| Homepage banner | **Homepage → Homepage banner** |
| Band page photo (the big photo at the top of the Band page) | **Settings → Site settings → Band photo (top of the Band page)** |
| Homepage tiles | **Homepage → Homepage tiles** (the "New merch" and "Latest release" tiles: leave Picture and Link empty to auto-show the newest merch / latest release, or fill them in to override) |
| Help, Terms, Privacy, Fans pages | **Fans → Pages** |
| Newsletter sign-ups | **Fans → Newsletter sign-ups** |
| FAQ / the chatbot's answers | **Fans → FAQ & chatbot answers** |
| Shop | **Shop → Merch (auto-synced)** |
| Band name, tagline, social links, chatbot | **Settings → Site settings** |

## 2. Logging in

1. Go to https://masalacoffee.in/cms/admin/.
2. Type your email and password, then click the blue **Sign In** button.

![The sign-in screen with the Sign In button circled](img/01-login.png)

*Type your email and password, then click **Sign In**. Shyam will give you
your login.*

- **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot Password** on this screen.
- **Want to change your password?** Click the person icon at the very bottom
  of the dark strip on the left.

## 3. Golden rules

> **Remember:** nothing is saved until you click the blue tick.

- **Save with the blue tick (✓) at the top right.** If you leave the page
  without clicking it, your work is gone.
- **Published means everyone can see it. Draft means only you can.**
  Use Draft while you are still writing.
- **Do not delete. Set it to Draft instead.** Deleting cannot be undone;
  Draft can.
- **Give the website a minute.** Changes show up within a minute. The chatbot
  can take up to five.
- **Ask before you change Site settings.** That one screen affects every page.

### 🎤 I want to add a show we just announced

**When to use this:** the band has confirmed a new concert and you want it on
the Tour page and the homepage.

**You will need:** the date, the city, the venue name, the ticket link, and
the poster picture saved on your computer or phone.

**Our example:** Kochi, Kerala, India · Saturday 14 February 2027 ·
Bolgatty Palace Lawns · tickets on BookMyShow.

1. In the sidebar, open **Tour** and click **Tour dates**.

   ![The sidebar with Tour dates circled](img/ex-01-1-sidebar.png)

   *Click **Tour dates** in the left-hand list.*

2. Click the **blue + button** at the top right.

   ![The blue plus button at the top right of the list](img/ex-01-2-new.png)

   *Click the blue **+** button. A blank show opens.*

3. Type the **City**, **State** and **Country**.

   ![City, State and Country filled in with Kochi, Kerala, India](img/ex-01-3-where.png)

   *City: `Kochi`. State: `Kerala`. Country: `India`.*

4. Click the **Date** box, move to the right month with the arrows, and click
   the day.

   ![The date picker open on February 2027 with the 14th circled](img/ex-01-4-date.png)

   *Pick **14** in **February 2027**. The time below the calendar is the show
   time — change it if you know it.*

5. Type the **Venue**.

   ![The Venue box with Bolgatty Palace Lawns typed in](img/ex-01-5-venue.png)

   *Venue: `Bolgatty Palace Lawns`. If the venue is not announced yet, leave
   it as "Venue to be announced".*

6. Paste the **Ticket URL**.

   ![The Ticket URL box with a BookMyShow link pasted in](img/ex-01-7-ticket.png)

   *Paste the whole BookMyShow (or other) link. Leave it empty if tickets are
   not on sale yet.*

   > The **Web address** field above the ticket URL fills itself in
   > automatically from the date, city and state as soon as you save — you
   > never need to type it. Leave it alone.

7. Click the **upload arrow** in the Poster picture box.

   ![The Poster picture box with the upload arrow circled](img/ex-01-8-poster.png)

   *Click the **↑** button, then pick the poster from your computer or phone.
   You can also drag the picture straight into the dotted box.*

8. Check the poster looks right.

   ![The poster showing inside the Poster picture box](img/ex-01-9-poster-done.png)

   *The poster now shows in the box. A tall (portrait) picture works best here.*

9. Fill in **Ticket status lines** — the small rows shown on the show card.

    ![The Ticket status lines box with one Tickets line typed in](img/ex-01-10-statuslines.png)

    *Copy this exactly and change the link:*

    ```json
    [
      { "label": "Tickets", "state": "available", "url": "https://..." }
    ]
    ```

    *`"state"` can be `available` (green "On sale"), `unavailable`
    ("Sold out") or `info`. Leave the box empty if you are not sure — the show
    still works.*

10. Click the **blue tick (✓)** at the top right to save.

    ![The blue tick save button at the top right](img/ex-01-11-save.png)

    *This is **Save**. Nothing is kept until you click it.*

#### What it looks like on the site

![The new Kochi show as a card on the Tour page](img/ex-01-12-tour-page.png)

*The Tour page now lists the show, with the poster, the date and the
"On sale" tag.*

![The homepage listing the new Kochi date](img/ex-01-13-home.png)

*It also appears in the tour list on the homepage.*

#### Common mistakes

- Leaving **Status** on something other than `Upcoming`.
- Using a wide photo as the poster — posters look best tall.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/tour/`.**

### 🎫 Tickets sold out, or the show is over

**When to use this:** tickets have gone, the show was cancelled, or the show
has already happened.

**You will need:** nothing. This is one field.

1. Open **Tour → Tour dates**, click the show, and change **Status**.

   ![The Status list open with Sold out circled](img/ex-02-1-status.png)

   *Click the **Status** box and pick one:*

   | Pick this | What happens on the website |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Sold out** | A "Sold out" tag shows; the ticket button stops working |
   | **Past** | The show leaves the Tour page and joins `/tour/past/` |
   | **Upcoming** | Back to normal, tickets on sale |

   *Then change **Ticket status lines** to match, for example:*

   ```json
   [
     { "label": "Sold out", "state": "unavailable" }
   ]
   ```

   *Click the blue tick (✓) to save.*

#### What it looks like on the site

![The Kochi card on the Tour page showing Sold out](img/ex-02-2-site.png)

*The card now says **Sold out** instead of **On sale**.*

#### Common mistakes

- Changing **Status** but leaving the old "Tickets — On sale" line in
  **Ticket status lines**. The card then says two different things.
- Deleting a finished show. Use **Past** — the archive and the setlists need it.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/tour/`.**

### 📸 I want to put up photos from last night

**When to use this:** you have pictures from a show and want a photo album on
the site, linked to that show.

**You will need:** the pictures on your computer, the album name, and the
show's web address (it filled itself in automatically when you saved the
show recipe above — open the show and copy it from the **Web address** box).

**Our example:** an album called "Live in Kochi, Feb 2027" with five pictures.

1. Open **Media → Photo albums** and click the **blue + button**.

   ![The Photo albums list with the blue plus button circled](img/ex-03-1-new.png)

   *Click the blue **+** button.*

2. Type the album **Title**.

   ![The Title box with Live in Kochi, Feb 2027 typed in](img/ex-03-2-title.png)

   *Title: `Live in Kochi, Feb 2027`. Then fill in **Category** (`Live`),
   **Date** and **Location** the same way as before.*

3. Pick a **Cover** picture — the one that shows on the Photos page.

   ![The file library open with the chosen cover picture circled](img/ex-03-3-cover.png)

   *Click the Cover box. The **File Library** opens. Click your picture, then
   the tick at the top right of the library.*

4. Type the show's web address into **Show Slug**.

   ![The Show Slug box with 2027-02-14-kochi-kerala typed in](img/ex-03-4-showlink.png)

   *`2027-02-14-kochi-kerala` — exactly what filled itself in on the show's
   own **Web address** box in the show recipe above. This is what links the
   album to the show.*

5. Scroll to **Photos** and click **Create New** — once for each picture.

   ![The Photos list with the Create New button circled](img/ex-03-5-photos.png)

   *Click **Create New**, drag one picture in, add a caption, save it, and
   repeat. You can drag the rows afterwards to change the order.*

6. Check all your pictures are listed.

   ![The Photos field showing five photos](img/ex-03-6-photos-added.png)

   *Five pictures, in order. Set **Status** to `Published` and click the blue
   tick (✓).*

#### What it looks like on the site

![The new photo album page on the website](img/ex-03-7-site-gallery.png)

*The album page at `/photos/live/live-in-kochi-feb-2027/`.*

![The photo strip on the show's own page](img/ex-03-8-site-show.png)

*Because you filled in **Show Slug**, the pictures also appear on the show's
own page.*

#### Common mistakes

- Forgetting **Status → Published**. A draft album is invisible.
- A typo in **Show Slug** — the photo strip then does not appear on the show.
- Uploading huge phone photos. See [Picture tips](#5-picture-tips).

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/photos/`.**

### 🎵 We released a new song

**When to use this:** a new single, EP or album is out on Spotify and Apple
Music and you want it on the Music page.

**You will need:** the song title, who wrote it, the release date, the cover
art, and the Spotify and Apple links.

**Our example:** a single called "Kochi Kaattu", out 10 February 2027.

1. First add the **song**. Open **Music → Songs & lyrics**, click the blue
   **+**, and fill in the title and who wrote it.

   ![The song form with Written by filled in](img/ex-04-1-song.png)

   *Title, web address and **Written by**. Set **Status** to `Published` and
   save with the blue tick.*

2. Now add the **release**. Open **Music → Releases**, click the blue **+**,
   and fill in the type, category and date.

   ![The release form showing Type set to Single](img/ex-04-2-details.png)

   *Type: `Single`. Category: `Singles`. Release date: `10 February 2027`.*

3. Pick the **Cover picture**.

   ![The file library with the release cover picture circled](img/ex-04-3-cover.png)

   *Click the Cover box, pick the picture in the File Library, then click the
   tick at the top right of the library.*

   > **Tip:** no artwork to hand? A YouTube video's picture works well. Open
   > `https://img.youtube.com/vi/<video ID>/maxresdefault.jpg` in your browser
   > (see the next recipe for where to find the video ID), right-click, and
   > save the picture.

4. Paste the streaming links.

   ![The Spotify URL box with a link pasted in](img/ex-04-4-links.png)

   *Paste the whole Spotify link into **Spotify URL**, the Apple Music link
   into **Apple URL**. Set **Status** to `Published` and save.*

5. Attach the song. Scroll to **Tracks**, click **Add Existing**, and click
   your song in the list.

   ![The song picker open with the new song's row circled](img/ex-04-5-tracks.png)

   *Click the row, then the tick at the top right. Drag the rows to set the
   track order. Save with the blue tick.*

#### What it looks like on the site

![The new single on the Releases page](img/ex-04-6-site.png)

*The release now appears on the Music page, with its cover and date.*

#### Common mistakes

- Adding the release but forgetting the song, so the album page has no
  tracklist.
- Putting the release in the wrong **Category** — it decides which shelf of
  the Music page it sits on.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/releases/`.**

### 🎧 I want to add a song to the site's music player

**When to use this:** you want a song to play in the slim music bar that sits
down the left-hand edge of the website and keeps playing while people click
around.

**You will need:** the audio file on your computer — an **MP3** or **M4A** — and
the right to put it online (see the warning below).

**Our example:** a live recording called "Kaantha (Live at Kochi)".

> ⚠️ **Only upload music the band owns.** Anything you put here plays to the
> whole internet, and the file itself can be downloaded. If a song is tied up
> in a label or publisher deal, **ask first** — or upload a 60-second preview
> instead of the whole track. When in doubt, don't.

1. Open **🎵 Music → Tracks (audio for the site player)** in the left-hand menu.
   This is the list of everything the player can play.

   ![The Directus menu with Tracks highlighted](img/ex-13-1-tracks-list.png)

   *You will see the band's ten songs, in the order they play, plus one greyed-out
   row called "[placeholder] Lo-fi demo loop" — that is the old stand-in tune,
   kept but switched off.*

2. Click the blue **+** at the top right. Fill in the **Title** (and the
   **Artist**, if it is not just "Masala Coffee"), then click the
   **Audio file** box and choose your MP3 from your computer.

   ![The new track form with the Audio file box highlighted](img/ex-13-2-new-track.png)

   *Only the **Title** and the **Audio file** are required. **Cover picture** is
   optional — if you leave it empty and pick a **Release**, the player borrows
   that release's cover.*

3. Set **Status** to `Published`, then save with the blue tick at the top right.

   ![The finished track with Status published and the save tick highlighted](img/ex-13-3-save.png)

   *Nothing appears on the website until the status is `Published` and you have
   pressed the tick.*

4. That's it — the song joins the ten already there.

   > **Tip:** the order of the list is the order it plays in. Drag the rows in
   > the **Tracks** list to change it. Today they run in order of how popular
   > each song is (Kaantha Njaanum Varaam first, then Munbe Vaa, then Aalayal
   > Thara Venam); if you would rather lead with a new single, just drag it to
   > the top.

   > **A note on how loud it is.** The ten songs already on the site were all
   > levelled to the same loudness, so none of them jumps out. A song you upload
   > yourself has not been through that, so it may sound noticeably louder or
   > quieter than the rest. If it does, ask whoever looks after the site to run
   > it through the same step.

#### What it looks like on the site

![The music player as a small play-button disc on the homepage, with a note explaining it click the small disc to play; hover it to open the full controls](img/ex-13-4-player.png)

*It starts as a small circle against the left edge, halfway down, showing
nothing but a play button — click it and it starts playing right there,
without opening anything. Hover (or, on a phone, tap the little arrow next to
it) to open the full bar: cover, back / play / next, the progress line and the
time; hovering it also slides out the song's name. It stays playing as
visitors move from page to page, and closes itself back down to the circle a
few seconds after they stop interacting with it, or if they press Escape. On a
phone it sits against the **right** edge instead, so it stays clear of the
band's name.*

*The coloured rings drifting behind the homepage follow the beat of whatever
is playing — automatically, a few minutes after you upload a song. There is
nothing to set up and nothing to fill in.*

#### Common mistakes

- Leaving **Status** on `Draft`, so the song never reaches the site.
- Uploading a video file or a WAV. It must be an **MP3** or **M4A**, or the
  player cannot play it.
- Uploading a very long, very large file. Keep it under about 10 MB so it
  starts quickly on a phone — the songs already on the site are 4–7 MB each.
- Re-publishing the old "[placeholder] Lo-fi demo loop" row by accident. Leave
  it on `Draft`.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website: press play in the bar on the left edge.**

### 🎬 We put a new video on YouTube

**When to use this:** a new video is live on YouTube and you want it on the
Videos page.

**You will need:** the YouTube link, and to know which collection it belongs
in (music videos, live sessions, or behind the scenes).

1. Find the **video ID** in the YouTube address.

   ![A YouTube address with the part after v= highlighted](img/ex-05-1-youtube-id.png)

   *In `youtube.com/watch?v=dj6-lQNKn1I` the ID is everything after **v=** —
   here `dj6-lQNKn1I`. On a short link like `youtu.be/dj6-lQNKn1I` it is the
   part after the last slash. It is always 11 characters.*

2. Open **Media → Videos**, click the blue **+**, and paste just the ID into
   **YouTube video ID**.

   ![The video form with the YouTube video ID box circled](img/ex-05-2-form.png)

   *Paste **only** the ID — not the whole address. Add a **Title** too.*

3. Choose the **Video set** the video belongs to.

   ![The video collection picker with Official music videos circled](img/ex-05-3-set.png)

   *Click the row for the collection you want, then the tick at the top right
   of the picker. Save with the blue tick.*

#### What it looks like on the site

![The new video on the Videos page](img/ex-05-4-site.png)

*The video appears in that collection on the Videos page.*

#### Common mistakes

- Pasting the whole `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…` address instead of
  just the ID. The video then shows as a grey box.
- Forgetting the **Video set** — the video exists but is on no page.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/videos/`.**

### 🏠 I want to change the big banner on the homepage

**When to use this:** the big pictures at the top of the homepage need
changing, or the button under them needs to point somewhere new.

**You will need:** a wide photo for each banner, the button words, and where
the button should link to.

The top of the homepage is now a **slideshow of your own photographs**, one
banner each, with the Masala Coffee logo and a button over the bottom corner.
Every banner needs a **landscape photo** — 2400 × 1350 or larger, JPG — and
the band's name, `Masala Coffee`, as its **Title**, which is what puts the
glowing logo on it. (Type anything else in Title and that text is shown
instead of the logo.) Three or four banners is a good number; more than about
five and visitors never see the last ones.

1. Open **Homepage → Homepage banner**, click the banner you want, and change
   **Button text** and **Button link**.

   ![The Button text and Button link boxes filled in](img/ex-07-1-button.png)

   *Button text: `Get Kochi tickets`. Button link: `/tour/2027-02-14-kochi-kerala/`.
   Leave both empty if you want no button at all. Save with the blue tick.*

2. To add a **second** banner, go back and click the blue **+**, then pick an
   **Image**.

   ![The file library with the banner picture circled](img/ex-07-2-image.png)

   *Click the Image box, pick a wide picture in the File Library, then the
   tick at the top right. The picture must be wider than it is tall —
   2400 × 1350 or larger is ideal, and it is shown across the whole screen,
   so use the good, big version of the photo rather than a small copy. Set
   **Title** to `Masala Coffee` so the logo goes on it, and write one short
   line in **Subtitle** — "Live across India", say.*

3. Set **Status** to **Published** so it actually shows.

   ![The Status box set to Published](img/ex-07-3-status.png)

   *`Draft` prepares a banner for later. `Archived` retires an old one
   without deleting it. Save with the blue tick.*

4. To change the **order**, drag the banners in the list.

   ![The list of banners with a drag handle circled](img/ex-07-4-reorder.png)

   *Grab the **≡** handle on the left of a row and drag it up or down. The top
   one shows first — it is the picture everybody sees, so put the best one
   there.*

   (**Homepage main photo** in **Settings → Site settings** is left over from
   the old homepage and is no longer shown anywhere. Ignore it.)

   The **Band photo** at the top of the Band page is a separate picture, set
   in **Settings → Site settings → Band photo (top of the Band page)**. It
   works best as a photo with a plain, flat-colour background, because the
   strip around the picture is painted to match — for the current photo that
   is a shade of yellow picked to match its own backdrop. Swapping in a photo
   with a different-coloured background needs a small code change too (ask
   the developer) so the strip still matches; swapping in another photo with
   the *same* yellow backdrop needs no code change. The picture is always
   shown in full (never cropped), so a wide group photo works best. Leave it
   empty to hide that part of the page entirely.

#### What it looks like on the site

#### Common mistakes

- A tall or square picture. It is shown across the whole screen, so the top
  and bottom get cropped away and faces get cut off.
- A small or blurry copy of a photo. Use the biggest version you have.
- A photo that is bright in the **bottom-right corner** — that is where the
  logo and the button sit. The site darkens that corner for you, but a photo
  that is busy there is still the hardest to read over.
- Forgetting the **Title**. Put `Masala Coffee` there for the logo.
- Leaving a banner on **Draft** and wondering why it never appears.
- A **Button link** with no leading slash. Use `/tour/`, not `tour/`.

> **Done ✅ — check it on the website at `/`.**

## 5. Picture tips

- **Format:** JPG or PNG.
- **Size:** phone photos are often 4000 pixels wide. Shrink them to about
  1600–2000 pixels wide before uploading. Big files make the site slow.
- **Homepage banner:** the picture **must be wider than it is tall**.
  1920 × 1080 is ideal. Keep faces away from the very edges.
- **Show posters:** tall, like a real gig poster.
- **News and release covers:** square or slightly wide. About 1200 × 800.
- **Member photos:** square or tall, centred on the face.

## 6. Troubleshooting

**My change is not showing on the website.**

1. Is **Status** set to `Published`? Draft and Archived are invisible.
2. Did you click the blue tick (✓)?
3. Hard-refresh the page: **Ctrl+Shift+R** (Windows and Linux) or
   **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac).
4. Give it a minute. The chatbot can take five.

**My new show is not on the Tour page.**

Check **Status** is set to `Upcoming` — the **Web address** box fills itself
in automatically, so that is not usually the problem.

**My picture looks cropped or squashed.**

The website crops pictures to fit a fixed shape. Check the shape against
[Picture tips](#5-picture-tips) — a tall picture in a wide slot always loses
its top and bottom.

**The chatbot does not know something.**

Add it to [FAQ & chatbot answers](#-the-chatbot-got-something-wrong). The
chatbot only knows what is in the admin panel.

**I broke something.**

Use [Revisions](#-oops--i-changed-the-wrong-thing).

**Still stuck?** Contact Shyam — especially for Site settings, the Pages
blocks, or anything that is not in this guide.

## 7. Words we use

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **CMS / admin panel** | The screen where you change the website. |
| **Collection** | A type of content in the sidebar: Tour dates, News & stories, Band members… |
| **Item** | One entry: one show, one post, one member. |
| **Field** | One box you fill in: Title, Date, Status. |
| **Save** | The blue tick (✓) at the top right. |
| **Status** | Whether something is live. See below. |
| **Web address / slug** | The end of the page's address. See below. |
| **File Library** | The store of every picture that has been uploaded. |
| **Revisions** | The history of saves, and how you undo a mistake. |

### What "Status" looks like

![The Status box set to Draft](img/ex-06-4-draft.png)

*`Draft` — saved, but nobody can see it.*

![The Status box set to Published](img/ex-06-5-published.png)

*`Published` — live on the website.*

### What a "web address" looks like

It fills itself in automatically — small letters, hyphens instead of spaces,
no punctuation. A show on 14 February 2027 in Kochi, Kerala becomes
`2027-02-14-kochi-kerala`, making the page `/tour/2027-02-14-kochi-kerala/`.
You never need to type it; you can still edit it afterwards if you ever need
to override it.
